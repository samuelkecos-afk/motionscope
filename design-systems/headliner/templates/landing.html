How it works

  1. Apply in five minutesTell us what you want to learn. No CV and no entry test.
  2. Talk to a teacherA 15-minute call to check the course fits your level and your week.
  3. Join your cohortTwo live classes a week with the same 12 people, plus short lessons you watch any time.
  4. Finish with a real projectReviewed by your teacher, with a certificate when you complete it.

Questions before you apply

What if I miss a live class?

Every class is recorded and posted within an hour.

How much time do I need?

Between 4 and 8 hours a week, depending on the course.

Do I need experience?

Beginner courses assume none. A short call checks your level for intermediate courses.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within the first 14 days for a full refund.
